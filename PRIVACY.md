# Privacy Policy for Finglish Keyboard

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Finglish Keyboard is designed to convert Finglish input into Persian text
without sending what you type to the developer.

## Data collection and transmission

Finglish Keyboard does not collect or transmit personal data. The app and
keyboard extension include no analytics, advertising, tracking, third-party
SDKs, or developer-operated network service. Conversion and ranking happen on
your device.

The keyboard does not request Full Access. Links in the containing app, such as
this privacy policy and the support page, open only when you choose them.

## On-device personalization

When you accept or reject a suggestion, the keyboard may save a weighted
association between the Finglish input and Persian choice. It may also save a
weighted association between a preceding Persian word and the next selected
word. These records can include words you typed or selected.

This personalization stays in the keyboard extension's local `UserDefaults`
storage. It is bounded to 500 input keys in each of the suggestion and next-word
stores, with up to 12 candidates per key. It is used only to rank future
suggestions on that device and is never sent to the developer or a third party.
Deleting Finglish Keyboard removes its locally stored app and extension data.

## Text access

iOS provides a limited amount of surrounding text so the keyboard can replace
the current word, suggest the next word, support undo, and move the cursor. The
keyboard processes that context in memory and does not transmit it.

## Children and third parties

The app is not directed at children for the purpose of collecting data, and it
does not sell or share data. Because no data is collected, there is no personal
data held by the developer to access, correct, or delete.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be published in this file with a revised
date. For privacy questions, use the contact route in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
